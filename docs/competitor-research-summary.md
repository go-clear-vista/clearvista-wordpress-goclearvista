# Competitor Research: Service Pages

Reviewed September 24, 2026 to guide the redesign of ClearVista's service pages. Sites reviewed: AVI-SPL (www.avispl.com), Ford AV (www.fordav.com) and CTI (www.cti.com).

## Findings

- **CTI** is a real AV integrator: designing, building and supporting AV systems since 1988, and a PSNI affiliate.
- **Ford AV** has a Salt Lake City office and targets Utah buyers directly, so it is a local competitor.
- **Ford AV has the most complete service pages.** Section order:
  1. Hero with one call-to-action button
  2. Grid of six benefits
  3. Process with named steps
  4. Deliverables and supported platforms
  5. Who the service is for
  6. Why choose Ford AV
  7. Downloadable brochure
  8. List of locations
  9. FAQ with 5–10 questions
  10. Contact form at the bottom
- **CTI has the most distinctive tone:** plain and reassuring, e.g. "This isn't a ticket queue" and "We support systems we didn't install."
- **Support plans:** AVI-SPL sells support as named tiers (Enhanced, Elite, Enterprise). CTI bundles a year of support with every install ("CTI Complete").
- **Experience centers:** none of the three has a page for a permanent experience center or showroom.
  - AVI-SPL mentions its Dallas office as a "workplace of the future" only inside a case study, and offers 3D virtual room tours with a "Schedule a demo" button.
  - Ford AV runs a yearly free event, "Innovate – A Technology Experience."
  - CTI has nothing comparable.

## How it shaped the ClearVista pages

- Every service page follows the Ford AV order, adapted: hero, stats bar, intro, six-card "what's included" grid, named process steps, certifications, a project spotlight, FAQ, links to the other services, and the demo/quote form.
- Copy uses CTI's plain, outcome-first voice and only facts already published by ClearVista (no invented statistics or guarantees).
- The Service Level Agreements page presents plans as a tier comparison table (AVI-SPL style), built from ClearVista's own plan PDF.
- The Experience Center is framed as a guided visit ("Book a Guided Visit"), never a showroom, which is a gap none of the three competitors fills.
