OUR STORY

From Television Pioneers to Integrated Technology

We’ve always grown with technology. Founded by Ken Bollinger as TV Specialists, we built our reputation on understanding new technology and making it work for the people who use it. That same mindset now powers professional audio-visual, security and communication systems for businesses, government institutions and higher education.

Today, as ClearVista, we help organizations communicate, educate, collaborate and inspire, with custom solutions, on-site consultations and long-term partnerships with industry-leading manufacturers.

Clarity in every connection. Vision for the future. We are ClearVista.

FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClearVista the same company as TVS Pro or TV Specialists, Inc.?

Yes. ClearVista is the evolution of TVS Pro and TV Specialists, Inc., continuing the same expertise in professional AV solutions with a renewed focus on communication and collaboration. Like TVS Pro, ClearVista is a DBA of TV Specialists: we are legally TV Specialists doing business as ClearVista.

Can I purchase products online?

Most of our products are not sold online, and some contracts don’t allow us to sell or list certain products online. For state and government purchasing we offer dedicated online portals, and we can set up additional portals for agencies integrated with Jaggaer/SciQuest. See our State of Utah Contract page.

Can I get a quote or a price on a product?

Yes. For the best pricing, quantity discounts and contract pricing, call us at (801) 486-5757 (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm MST) for the quickest response, or use the contact form below.

Can ClearVista order products that are not normally carried?

Yes. In most cases we can order anything within the product lines and brands we carry and are authorized for. Sometimes an extra cost or a shipping delay may apply.

Do you offer repair services?

We provide support and service on products and systems purchased from ClearVista. Due to industry changes and the cost of repairing AV equipment, we no longer repair products purchased elsewhere.

Does ClearVista buy or sell used equipment?

No. However, we sometimes sell showroom and demo models at a lower cost. Call (801) 486-5757 if you’re interested.
